绿能充电 Design System

Semantic Design Language for EV Charging Infrastructure

1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

This design system embodies an atmosphere of energetic optimism and environmental responsibility. The visual language combines the clarity of modern technology with the warmth of sustainable living. Clean lines and generous whitespace create a sense of breathability, while the vibrant color palette communicates the dynamic energy of electric mobility. The overall aesthetic is forward-looking yet approachable, reflecting a brand that is both innovative and user-centric.

2. Color Palette & Roles

4. Component Stylings

Buttons

Buttons follow a consistent pattern with 8px border radius, medium weight text, and smooth hover transitions. Primary buttons use the Sky Blue color, while secondary actions use outline or ghost variants.

Cards / Containers

Cards feature 8px rounded corners, white backgrounds, and subtle borders. Hover states add elevation through shadow depth increase and border color change.
